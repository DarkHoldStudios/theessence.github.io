WHO WE ARE?

More than music. A dark atmosphere built from emotion, noise and identity.

THE ESSENCE is an independent band focused on creating immersive experiences through music, visuals and emotion. Every project performance and composition is part of a larger artistic universe.

Charlotte Rodríguez - Lead Vocalist

Melodic vocals driven by passion, warmth and emotionally uplifting energy.

Juan Castillo - Rhythm Guitarist

Aggressive rhythmic guitars blending raw energy, melodic tension and powerful live presence.

Jonatán Torres - Drummer

Powerful drums blending chaos, precision and relentless stage energy.

Cristian Vergara - Bassist

Bass driven by intensity, chaos and the emotional weight behind every song.

Erik Marin - Principal Guitarist

Atmospheric lead guitar shaped by experimentation, aggression and dark melodic textures.
